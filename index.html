Home Health Nurse Routing Scheduling and Optimization

A predict-then-optimize solution for efficient home health nurse scheduling and routing.

Healthcare Python Optimization
About

This course project answers the following question: for a home health agency serving Allegheny County, PA, how should weekly patient visits be assigned and sequenced across nurses, how many nurses of each skill type are required, and what is the operational cost of guaranteeing continuity of care? We developed a predict-then-optimize solution that first forecasts patient demand, and then optimizes in two layers. An assignment model in Gurobi decides which nurse sees which patient on which day. A sequencing model then finds, for each nurse-day, the cheapest order of visits that gets the nurse home before their shift ends..

My role

Conducted data cleaning and standardization, developed the two-layer optimization algorithm and sequencing model in Python, and documented the process in the final report.

SAFE: Sepsis Anticipation and Flagging Engine

A clinical decision support prototype for early sepsis detection in ICU settings.

Healthcare ML Python PostgreSQL Docker
About

SAFE flags ICU patients at risk of sepsis earlier than reactive, clinician-dependent workflows allow. The system pairs a PostgreSQL relational backend with an ML risk-prediction model and a role-based interface featuring tiered alerts, transparent model explanations, and structured override logging so clinicians stay in the loop without alert fatigue. It is built using the PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2019 dataset, with EDA, modeling, and sensitivity analysis end-to-end.

My role

Conducted EDA and sensitivity analysis, designed and developed the complete front-end, and documented the process in the final report.

HospiTrack: Midwest ER Wait Tracker

A web app for U.S. Midwest patients to find nearby emergency departments and walk-in clinics, with live wait times, quality scores, and patient experience metrics.

Healthcare Python Streamlit Web
About

A tool to help patients navigate urgent care decisions in the moment. It compares nearby EDs and walk-in clinics on wait time, care quality, and patient experience so people can make faster, better-informed choices. Patients can: enter or select their location on a map, view nearby emergency facilities with their estimated wait times, sort hospitals by ED efficiency, patient satisfaction, or mortality performance, and adjust quality rankings based on chief complaints (e.g., Stroke, Heart Attack, Pneumonia).
